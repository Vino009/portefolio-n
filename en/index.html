Barbershop management app

Business web app for bookings, clients and services

Role
Full-stack development
Stack
  • Laravel
  • React
  • MySQL
  • Tailwind CSS

A shop that handles bookings by hand loses appointments, loses track of its clients and has no clear view of the services it delivers.

The solution

A web app that brings the whole shop's activity into one place: a React front end, with business logic and data handled by Laravel and MySQL.

  • Appointment booking and scheduling
  • Client records and service history
  • User administration
  • Secure authentication with roles (admin, barber)

Technical decisions

A MySQL schema designed to keep appointments, clients, services and barbers consistent, plus role-based access so each user only sees what's relevant to them.

How I work

A clear process, even remotely: at every stage you know what's done, what's next, and what I need from you.

  1. Discovery

    A first call to understand your needs, your users and your constraints.

  2. Scope

    We agree in writing on the features and deliverables.

  3. Technical proposal

    I propose an architecture, a step-by-step delivery plan and an estimate.

  4. Iterative development

    Regular releases you can test, with a progress update at each milestone.

  5. Testing and sign-off

    Features are checked and issues fixed before going live.

  6. Launch and support

    Deployment, handover of code and documentation, and support after launch.

Terms, timelines and budget are set for each project, based on its scope.
